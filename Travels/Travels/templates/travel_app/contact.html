Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance for domestic trips and 6–8 weeks for international packages to secure the best availability and pricing.
Can I customize a package?
Absolutely! All our packages can be tailored to your preferences — dates, accommodation type, activities, and more. Contact us with your requirements.
What is the cancellation policy?
Pending bookings can be cancelled anytime for a full refund. Confirmed bookings follow the individual package policy — typically 50–100% refund depending on notice period.
Do you offer group discounts?
Yes! Groups of 8 or more travelers receive special pricing. Contact our team directly for a custom quote tailored to your group size and destination.
